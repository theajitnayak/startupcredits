# Catalog review on 2026-10-15

Source inventory: 85 entries. Prepared reviewed catalog: 52 entries. Held outside publication: 33 entries. Three providers explicitly say applications are closed.

The reviewer fetched the listed source for every entry. A successful response was not treated as verification. Generic homepages, unreadable terms and inadequately established claims were excluded. The included entries were reviewed for identity, benefit and the listed criteria. Full provider eligibility is not claimed.

The dollar-valued equivalents and aggregate available-credit totals were removed rather than treating plan retail prices or selection ceilings as awarded credits. Every retained entry has the actual review date, an official link, a short locating excerpt and a source digest. The private source snapshots are excluded from Git and publication.

Confirmed corrections include Vercel and Upstash open source applications being closed, Anthropic's current startup cohort being full and Google's Start-tier age limit being twenty-four months on its current tier page. Program availability is separate from an individual applicant's approval or rejection.

`npm test` covers publication rejection, date integrity, lookalike domains, missing versus zero profile values and conservative fit results. `node scripts/check-catalog.mjs YYYY-MM-DD` verifies the publication policy with the actual date.

The previous broad star thresholds and guaranteed-sounding claims were removed. The detailed engine currently encodes conditions for eight programs. It is a precheck and always retains provider review as a prerequisite.

The site design is left for the owner to review. This branch includes only minimal functional and truth-related text changes alongside the new catalog and product logic. It has not been merged or deployed.

Source snapshots and held entries stay in the local, Git-ignored review folder. They are excluded from the public dataset and pull request.
